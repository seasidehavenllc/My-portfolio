Questions, answered.

Pricing, revamps, templates, timelines and what happens after launch. Still wondering? Text or call us anytime.

Pricing & payment

How do your prices compare to Wix or Squarespace?

A DIY plan on Wix runs $17 to $159 a month and Squarespace $19 to $99, and you build it yourself. Pros hired through the Wix Marketplace list starting prices from about $75 to $2,000. Our template sites start at $799 and custom sites at $2,499, built entirely for you, and you're not locked into a platform. See the full comparison.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. You get a fixed price in writing before we start. The only ongoing costs are your domain name (usually about $15 a year) and optional hosting & care from $29 a month.

How do payments work?

50% to begin and 50% at launch.

Can I pay online?

Yes. Pick a package on the pricing page and check out securely with Stripe. Template sites and polishes are paid in full; bigger builds start with a 50% deposit, and the balance is invoiced at launch.

Do I have to pay monthly?

No. Hosting & care is optional. You can host anywhere, and we'll hand over everything.

Revamps

What qualifies for revamp pricing?

Any existing website we can pull content from, on any platform. Because we reuse your words, photos and structure, a full revamp is 40% less than a new build.

What's the difference between Polish and Revamp?

Polish ($299) keeps your current site and platform and fixes the look, speed, mobile layout and SEO basics. Revamp ($1,499) is a full redesign rebuilt from scratch with your existing content.

Will I lose my Google rankings?

No. We map every old page to its new address with redirects so search engines and bookmarks follow along.

Can you revamp a Wix or Squarespace site?

Yes. We can polish it right where it is, or move you to faster hosting you own.

Templates

Are your templates really original?

Yes. Every template is designed and coded in-house by our studio. We don't resell theme-store designs.

Will other businesses have the same site?

Your colors, fonts, logo, photos and words make it yours. If you want something nobody else has, our Business package is designed from a blank page.

Can I switch templates later?

Yes. Since your content is separate from the design, switching is a small project rather than a rebuild.

Process & support

How long does it take?

Launch sites go live in about a week, custom Business sites in 3 to 4 weeks, Growth sites in 5 to 7 weeks and revamps in 2 to 3 weeks.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, a few photos (or we'll source them), and 15 minutes to tell us about your business. We can write your copy too.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain, content and code belong to you. If you ever leave, everything goes with you.

Can I update it myself?

Yes. We can set up easy editing, or simply text us your changes on a care plan.
